## What I’m Learning

In this project, I’m learning how real-world data moves through a RAG pipeline, starting from raw documents and eventually reaching retrieval and generation.

I’m experimenting with different data sources such as text files, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, JSON, websites, databases, and transcripts. Along the way, I’m focusing on the practical problems that come with real-world data — parsing inconsistencies, messy content, metadata handling, and preparing information in a form that can be effectively chunked, embedded, and retrieved.

The goal is to understand not just how to use RAG frameworks, but what is actually happening underneath them and how each stage affects the quality of the final system.
